## National framework

### Republic Act 8550, Philippine Fisheries Code, as amended

- **Issuer / date:** Congress of the Philippines; approved 25 February 1998. Republic Act 10654 later amended the Code.
- **Source / locator:** [Supreme Court E-Library, RA 8550](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4002), especially Sections 16–18 and 90; read with [RA 10654](https://issuances-library.senate.gov.ph/legislative%2Bissuances/Republic%20Act%20No.%2010654). The base text describes municipal/city fisheries-management responsibilities and municipal-water fishing provisions.
- **Application to Bangwit:** Framework reference only. Any user-facing result needs the applicable amended text, implementing orders, exact place, user category, species, gear and date.
- **Open:** No legal review of the consolidated current Code has been completed for this product. Do not infer that a recreational visitor may fish from these sections alone.
- **Reuse:** Public display and quotation terms have not been separately reviewed; link to the source and paraphrase if used.

### BFAR Fisheries Administrative Order 232, series of 2010

- **Issuer / date:** Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources; adopted 24 March 2010.
- **Source / locator:** [Supreme Court E-Library, FAO 232](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50770), Sections 1–5 and 7.
- **Source-text summary:** Establishes a limited-access licensing policy for **commercial fishing** in Manila Bay. Its definition of commercial fishing excludes subsistence and sports fishing; its prohibitions concern commercial fishing without the specified license or contrary to its license conditions.
- **Application to Bangwit:** Do not label this a blanket restriction for recreational or sports fishing. It does not by itself establish permission for those activities.
- **Open:** Section 7 states that the order takes effect after publication and registration. This review did not verify those occurrence facts, later changes, or interaction with other applicable national and local rules for Bangwit.
- **Reuse:** Link and paraphrase; public-display terms have not been separately reviewed.

### BFAR Fisheries Administrative Order 201-1, 2025 amendment

## Cavite local instruments

### Bacoor City Ordinance 278-2023 — Comprehensive Fishery Ordinance

- **Issuer / date:** Sangguniang Panlungsod of Bacoor; enacted 24 April 2023 and approval date shown as 2 May 2023 in the PDF.
- **Source / locator:** [Bacoor City Sangguniang Panlungsod PDF](https://bacoorcitysp.com/wp-content/PDF/ORD/2023/278%202023.pdf?_t=1684824815), Sections 3, 7, 18, 64 and 71. The text covers Bacoor municipal waters and fishery resources; Section 7 was later amended by Ordinance 292-2023.
- **Source-text summary:** The document contains local fisheries-management provisions, including registration/permit and gear-related sections. Section 71 says the ordinance takes effect after posting in public/conspicuous places and barangay halls for at least three consecutive weeks after approval.
- **Open:** Evidence that the specified posting occurred, any later amendment/repeal beyond the identified amendment, present administrative application, map version and GIS reference system, and public-app reuse terms have not been established. A source copy alone is not proof that every clause is currently operative.
- **Application to Bangwit:** No fishing-permission answer or mapped legal zone is derived from this card.

### Bacoor City Ordinance 292-2023 — amendment to Section 7

- **Issuer / date:** Sangguniang Panlungsod of Bacoor; issued 26 July 2023 according to the UP Law Center NARO entries.
- **Source / locator:** [Bacoor City PDF](https://bacoorcitysp.com/wp-content/PDF/ORD/2023/292%202023.pdf?_t=1697086297), Section 1 and Section 3; [NARO record 23-1071](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/2024); [NARO record 24-0139](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/3261).
- **Source-text summary:** Amends Section 7’s municipal-water zonation and includes waypoint tables for named zones. The PDF says it takes effect 15 days after publication once weekly for three consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation within Bacoor and posting in three conspicuous places.
- **Open:** Proof of the required publication and posting was not found. NARO exposes two records for the same titled ordinance with different filing dates/registration numbers; the reason for the duplicate records is unresolved. The PDF does not establish a CRS/datum for the waypoint tables. Current zone interpretation, later changes and reuse permission remain unverified.
- **Application to Bangwit:** Coordinates must not be treated as a verified legal polygon or point-in-polygon rule without authoritative mapping/technical confirmation.

### Bacoor City Ordinance 438-2025 — Revised Environment Code

- **Issuer / dates:** Bacoor Sangguniang Panlungsod; council passage on 5 May 2025 (page 94), with mayoral approval stamped 16 May 2025 (page 95). Neither date is established here as the effective date.
- **Source / locator:** [Final 95-page City Council PDF](https://bacoorcitysp.com/wp-content/PDF/ORD/2025/438-2025.pdf), Article VII, Sections 3–5 (pages 67–75), and Article XIII, Sections 3–4 (page 94). Review used the final ordinance, not the separately indexed public-hearing draft. Retrieved on 17 October 2026; 12,169,274 bytes; SHA-256 `fee667385d48e2a057a8654f8b780e3c7d57cf9cecaba58f2b504e08e5d16d32`.
- **Source-text scope:** Article VII addresses MPA/NTZ designation, zone restrictions, coastal planning and quotas. The catch-and-release provision is within Section 3(E)(c)(ii)(2), concerning recreational zones within MPAs and further City guidelines; it is not evidence of blanket permission throughout Bacoor. Section 4 calls for coastal zoning plans, and Section 5 calls for quotas to be determined and published; no actual current zone or quota is established by this review.
- **Effectivity / relationship:** Article XIII conditions effectivity on ten days from posting in three conspicuous places within Bacoor and publication at least once in a newspaper of general circulation within Cavite. Its general repeal clause concerns inconsistent provisions. A whole-document text search found no numbered reference to 278-2023 or 292-2023; that does not establish their continuing validity or repeal.
- **Open / application:** Publication/posting proof, current MPA designations/maps, implementing guidelines, quota notices, reconciliation with the earlier fishery ordinances, later changes and reuse remain unresolved. Section 3's activity/penalty provisions need contextual review together. Keep this as an internal reference; no app permission, legal polygon or quota is enabled.
