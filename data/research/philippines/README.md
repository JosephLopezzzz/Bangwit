# Philippines fish research workspace

Research and local app slices implemented 22-24 October 2026. This directory contains **research inventories, frozen candidates and scoped review decisions**. The Species page now has four Taal Lake checklist assertions, three historical Taal Lake CAS specimen-locality assertions, 12 Palico-Lian River survey-table assertions, one historical text-locality assertion for Molawin Creek, one dated Laguna de Bay specimen assertion, four historical Pansipit River specimen-locality assertions, one 1929 Angat Dam/Angat River museum-specimen locality assertion, and three 1982 Bulacan Manila Bay north-shore fish-trap specimen assertions. The Laguna record is restricted to noncommercial CC BY-NC 3.0 use and uses the corrected Calahan locality. The five article/report leads in the earlier coverage-sweep follow-up yielded zero new assertions; the Pansipit and Caliraya/Lumot GBIF checks were reviewed separately. No regional baseline is complete or released. Follow the [expansion plan](../../../docs/PHILIPPINES_SPECIES_EXPANSION_PLAN.md).
