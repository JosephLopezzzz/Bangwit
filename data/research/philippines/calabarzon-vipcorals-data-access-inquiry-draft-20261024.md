# VIP CORALS data-access inquiry — draft only

**Status:** Prepared for Joseph Lopez to review and send. **Not sent.**

**To:** vipcorals@g.batstate-u.edu.ph

**Subject:** Inquiry on Verde Island fish catalogue data and reuse terms for Bangwit

Dear VIP CORALS team,

My name is Joseph Lopez, and I am developing **Bangwit**, an independent personal project. Bangwit is planned to cover the Philippines in stages—Luzon, then the Visayas, then Mindanao. It is currently an early CALABARZON research prototype. The project aims to present source-attributed information about fish in marine, estuarine and freshwater habitats. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Batangas State University or VIP CORALS.

I read the Marine Repository Hub page and its description of the Verde Island survey and specimen collection. I understand that the electronic catalogue is still being developed and that the 2020 paper's count of 44 fish refers to specimens, not necessarily 44 different species.

Could you let me know whether a fish catalogue, inventory or dataset from the Verde Island collections or survey is available, and what process I should follow to request access? If shareable, the most useful fields for accurate, source-linked records would be:

- the scientific name as recorded, identification authority or determiner, and any later taxonomic updates;
- specimen or catalogue identifier and the associated collection event;
- collection date and reported locality or station, with coordinates and spatial precision only if release is permitted;
- habitat or environment fields, if recorded; and
- the dataset version, citation and recommended attribution.

Because Bangwit is intended to become a public web app, could you also clarify the applicable terms for displaying names and factual record details, publishing derived summaries, redistribution, and any commercial use. Please distinguish those data terms from permissions for specimen photographs; I will not reuse the hub's images unless separately authorized. If precise localities or particular records should remain restricted, I can omit or generalize them.

If the catalogue is not yet available, a rough timeline or the correct office and request process would be helpful. Please also let me know whether there are required forms, fees or attribution wording before I proceed.

Thank you for your time and for maintaining the repository.

Sincerely,

Joseph Lopez

Bangwit — independent project
