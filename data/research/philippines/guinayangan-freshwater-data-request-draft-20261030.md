## CLSU Freshwater Aquaculture Center

**To:** `fac@clsu.edu.ph`

**Subject:** Inquiry about fish-survey records from the Guinayangan small-scale aquaculture project

Dear Freshwater Aquaculture Center team,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am developing Bangwit, an independent early-stage project intended to provide carefully sourced fish and fishing information for the Philippines. The national scope is a long-term direction; regional coverage is still incomplete, and I am currently checking research leads in Guinayangan, Quezon.

The CLSU article about the 2024 Guinayangan action-research visit mentions seven lagoons, local aquaculture farms, tilapia fingerling distribution to smallholders, and a potential Fish Conservation Zone in Maulawin Spring Protected Landscape. I have not treated those statements as a wild-fish survey, and I have not equated the protected landscape with the separately named Maulawin Itaas and Maulawin Ibaba water-supply springs in the Guinayangan Water District report.

Could you please advise whether the project produced or can point me to any publicly shareable fish inventory, survey table, report, or monitoring dataset for the Guinayangan sites? In particular, I would appreciate:

- the names of the seven visited lagoons and whether any are associated with a specific river, spring, or fishpond;
- any actual fish observations or survey results, distinguished from aquaculture stocking or farm production;
- available dates, sampling methods, station/locality descriptions, taxonomic identifications, and coordinate precision that may be shared;
- a public report, repository link, DOI, or other recommended citation;
- the license or written conditions for paraphrasing or displaying any results in a public app; and
- the appropriate IIRR, LGU, or other project contact if the underlying material is held elsewhere.

I will not infer species presence from fingerling releases, infer absence from a zero database search, or publish restricted site coordinates. If the information is not reusable in a public app, a citation or public link alone would still help me document the coverage gap accurately.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,

Joseph Lopez
