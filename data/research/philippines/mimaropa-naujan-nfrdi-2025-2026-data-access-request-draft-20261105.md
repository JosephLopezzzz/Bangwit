# Draft inquiry — not sent

**To:** DA-NFRDI Freshwater Fisheries Research and Development Center / Capture Fisheries Research and Development Division
**Subject:** Inquiry on releasable Lake Naujan stock-assessment species list and data terms

Magandang araw.

I am Joseph Lopez, building Bangwit as an independent personal project: a staged, Philippines-wide fish explorer and private catch journal. The project is not an NFRDI, BFAR, DENR, LGU, or university product. We are trying to cite reliable sources and keep survey findings separate from current distribution, fishing rules, and personal catch reports.

I read NFRDI's October 2026 summary of the Lake Naujan stock assessment conducted from July 2025 to June 2026. It reports 43 documented aquatic taxa, estimated fisheries production, catch rates, and reproductive-biology work on paitan (*Barbodes hemictenus*). The public summary does not show the complete taxon list or the supporting methods and locality details.

Could you please advise whether NFRDI can share a public report, presentation, or data table for this assessment? If available, the most useful fields for careful source attribution would be:

- the names of all 43 aquatic taxa, with the original taxon wording and any accepted-name crosswalk;
- whether the 43 entries are all fishes or include other aquatic groups;
- sampling dates and methods, and whether each record comes from a landing center, lake survey, tributary, or the Butas River;
- the names of stations or landing centers and which findings are lake-wide versus site-specific;
- the basis and units for estimated production and catch rates, with any species-by-site or species-by-period breakdown suitable for public release;
- available specimen, photograph, or taxonomic verification references for the paitan work; and
- applicable citation, attribution, redistribution, and public-app reuse terms for the report and any structured data.

We will preserve the results as historical, study-scoped evidence and will not present aggregate catch figures as a complete lake checklist or as fishing, legal, or safety advice. If some information is not releasable, a public report or description of the permitted fields would still help identify a suitable citation and prevent overstatement.

Thank you for your guidance.

Joseph Lopez
Bangwit (independent personal project)
