I reviewed your article, “Diet Composition and Feeding Habits of Naujan White Goby (*Glossogobius aureus*) in Naujan Lake, Philippines” (BIOTROPIA 32(2), 2025; DOI: 10.11598/btb.2025.32.2.2450). I noticed that the article’s License section identifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, while the journal page footer also mentions CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and links to CC BY 4.0.

Could you please clarify:

1. Which license and version govern this specific article and its diet results?

5. What attribution wording and any other conditions should Bangwit follow? If another rights holder must grant permission, please point me to the correct contact.

I have not copied the article’s diet table or percentages into Bangwit, and no app projection is planned until the terms are clear.

Thank you for your time and for the study. I would be glad to provide a concise mockup of the proposed attribution and display if useful.
