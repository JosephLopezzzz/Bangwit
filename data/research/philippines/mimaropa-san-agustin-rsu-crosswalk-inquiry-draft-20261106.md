# RSU San Agustin specimen and station crosswalk inquiry

**Status:** Draft only. Not sent. Use the corresponding author's contact address listed in the article when sending. This draft contains no personal email address.

**Subject:** Request for specimen and station crosswalk for the 2022 RSU San Agustin fish survey

Dear Dr. Gonzalez and co-authors,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am developing Bangwit, an independent personal project intended to organize useful, sourced fishing and fish-biodiversity information across the Philippines. The current pilot is Cavite. Bangwit is not affiliated with Romblon State University, and I am treating the 2025 San Agustin article as a research lead only; I have not added its fish records to a public species map.

I am reviewing your paper, “Composition and Longitudinal Distribution of Fish Assemblages in the Estuary and Tributary Systems in RSU San Agustin, Romblon, Philippines,” to avoid assigning a species to the wrong specimen, station, or waterbody. If available, could you please clarify or share:

1. Whether the 2022 specimens are still held by the RSU San Agustin Campus Biological Repository, and whether each specimen has a catalogue, voucher, or accession number that can be linked to the Table 1 label.
2. What named waterbody and reach each Table 2 site code A–F refers to, and whether a station map or site-coordinate table exists.
3. The intended format and datum for the methods coordinates, which give approximately `12.6010, 122.1924` and also the parenthetical text `12.36' North 122.8 East`. Please confirm whether either is a general campus reference point or an actual sampling station.
4. Whether diagnostic photographs, identification notes, or sequence accessions linked directly to the vouchers are available for labels still requiring verification, especially the genus-only *Rhinogobius* record.
5. Any attribution, citation, permission, or reuse conditions for the specimen-to-station crosswalk and locality metadata.

This is a request for metadata and clarification only; I am not requesting specimens, samples, or access to restricted facilities. I will not present a crosswalk or local occurrence as verified until its source and reuse conditions are clear. If a formal request through RSU or a campus office is required, please let me know the appropriate process.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,  
Joseph Lopez
