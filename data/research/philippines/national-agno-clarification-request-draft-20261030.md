# Draft inquiry to the Agno River Basin study's corresponding author

**Status: unsent.** The corresponding-author address below is printed in the article. No email or repository correction request has been sent.

**To:** kitfelian.tenio@vsu.edu.ph  
**Subject:** Clarification on season labels and sampling-site localities in the Agno River Basin fish dataset

Dear Dr. Tenio,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am independently developing Bangwit, a fishing information project intended to grow toward Philippines-wide coverage. It is still in research/prototype development. I am reviewing your 2026 Agno River Basin fish study and the related University of Glasgow CC BY 4.0 dataset so that any research notes retain the source's original wording and limitations. I am not affiliated with the study or your institutions, and I will not imply endorsement.

Could you please help clarify three source details?

1. **Season and month mapping.** The Glasgow record and its README map wet season to January 2022 and dry season to July 2021. Article section 2.2 appears to map July to wet and January to dry. The `Fish_count.csv` contains only `Wet`/`Dry` columns without dates. Which month corresponds to each fish-count season label? Is there a corrected README, dataset version, or article notice?
2. **ARB5 location.** Table 1 and `Sampling_sites.csv` identify ARB5 as “San Roque, San Manuel, Pangasinan,” at WGS84 16.1056 N, 120.7019 E, with a land-use note of “San Roque Dam outflow.” A preliminary point screen using a derived municipal-boundary service and an OpenStreetMap-based reverse geocoder returns San Nicolas, Pangasinan. Those map sources are not authoritative and may be imprecise, so I am not treating this as proof of an error. Could you confirm whether the coordinate and locality text are correct, and clarify the station's municipality/reach or provide an authoritative station map if one is available?
3. **ARB4 name.** The data README/CSV says “Dalapurip,” while the article says “Dalupirip.” Are these alternate spellings for the same sampling locality, or should one be corrected?

Until clarified, I am keeping the wet/dry month mapping unassigned and preserving the locality spellings as printed. I will not infer a current distribution, access point, or river reach from the historical station data.

Thank you for your time. If another author or repository contact is better placed to answer, I would appreciate being directed to them.

Best regards,  
Joseph Lopez
