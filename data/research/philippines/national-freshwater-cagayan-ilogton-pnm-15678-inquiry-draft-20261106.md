# Draft inquiry: *Ilogton ilogton* holotype PNM 15678

**Status:** Not sent. Review and send manually if you want a response from the collection steward.

**To:** `zoology@nationalmuseum.gov.ph`

**Cc:** `rccmd@nationalmuseum.gov.ph`

**Subject:** Catalog and locality inquiry for *Ilogton ilogton*, holotype PNM 15678

Good day,

I am Joseph Lopez, developing Bangwit, an independent early-stage project intended to document fish and fishing-related information across the Philippines. We are currently reviewing historical freshwater fish records from the Cagayan River basin. No occurrence or location claim will be published from this inquiry unless it is verified and its reuse conditions are clear.

The Catalog of Fishes cites *Ilogton ilogton* Endruweit, 2024, with type locality “Cagayan River, Muta village, Kasibu, Nueva Vizcaya” and holotype PNM 15678. Could the Zoology Division please advise, if these details are available:

1. Is PNM 15678 the current collection number and holotype record? If the collection uses a different current acronym or catalog number, could you clarify it?
2. What is the exact locality wording on the specimen label, and are collector, collection date, coordinates, datum, or coordinate uncertainty recorded?
3. Is there a type-series list or another authoritative record that links this specimen to Muta and the Cagayan River?
4. May researchers consult Endruweit (2024), *Fishes of the Red River – Perciformes, Synbranchiformes*, page 204 and figures 112, 298–299, through the NMP library or collection?
5. What attribution and permission conditions apply if we cite the catalog metadata or request a label image for internal verification?

Bangwit is currently an independent project and is not affiliated with a school or university. Please let me know if a signed institutional endorsement is required even for a catalog/label inquiry, or if there is a suitable public catalog or form we should use instead. We are not requesting restricted specimen access or permission to republish images without approval.

Thank you for your time.

Joseph Lopez (`[reply-to email, if you choose to include one]`)

The NMP lists its Zoology Division at `zoology@nationalmuseum.gov.ph` and local 3003. Its official [services page](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/services/) describes the division's animal biodiversity and taxonomy inquiry scope and provides the relevant collection/research request guidance.
