# Draft inquiry: Cagayan freshwater fish type localities and PNM specimens

**Status:** Not sent. This combines two catalog/label questions for one NMP inquiry; review and send manually if you want a response from the collection steward.

**To:** `zoology@nationalmuseum.gov.ph`

**Cc:** `rccmd@nationalmuseum.gov.ph`

**Subject:** Catalog, specimen-label, and type-locality inquiry for two Cagayan freshwater fishes

Good day,

I am Joseph Lopez, developing Bangwit, an independent early-stage project intended to document fish and fishing-related information across the Philippines. We are reviewing historical freshwater fish records associated with the Cagayan River drainage. No species occurrence or location claim will be published from these historical type records unless it is verified and its reuse conditions are clear.

The Catalog of Fishes cites two Endruweit (2024) records: *Ilogton ilogton*, reported from “Cagayan River, Muta village, Kasibu, Nueva Vizcaya” with holotype PNM 15678; and *Glossogobius biorbitalis*, reported from “Bua River, Cagayan River drainage, Dupax Del Norte County, Nueva Vizcaya” with holotype PNM 15688. Could the Zoology Division please advise, if these details are available:

1. Are PNM 15678 and PNM 15688 the current collection identifiers and holotype records? If either has a different current collection acronym or catalog number, could you clarify it?
2. What is the exact locality wording on each specimen label? Are collector, collection date, coordinates, datum, coordinate precision, or uncertainty recorded for either specimen?
3. Is there an authoritative type-series list or collection record linking PNM 15678 to Muta/Cagayan River and PNM 15688 to Bua River/Cagayan drainage?
4. For *G. biorbitalis*, can the Museum clarify whether “Dupax Del Norte County” is a locality description, an administrative qualifier, or a transcription issue? PSA's current PSGC lists Barangay Bua under Kasibu and does not list a Bua barangay among Dupax del Norte's 15 barangays. I do not assume these records identify the river or prove the catalog wording is incorrect; any label or locality context would help distinguish the names.
5. May researchers consult Endruweit (2024), *Fishes of the Red River – Perciformes, Synbranchiformes*, pages 204 and 199 and their cited figures, through the NMP library or collection?
6. What attribution and permission conditions apply if we cite catalog metadata or request a label image for internal verification?

Bangwit is currently an independent project and is not affiliated with a school or university. Please let me know if a signed institutional endorsement is required even for a catalog/label inquiry, or if there is a suitable public catalog or form we should use instead. We are not requesting restricted specimen access or permission to republish images without approval.

Thank you for your time.

Joseph Lopez (`[reply-to email, if you choose to include one]`)
