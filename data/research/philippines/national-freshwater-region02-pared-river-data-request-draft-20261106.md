# Unsent draft: Pared River Catchment fish-survey data inquiry

**Status:** Draft only. Not sent. Verify the official recipient before sending.

**To:** Darwin Initiative project 31-005 / ZSL Philippines project team; DA-BFAR Region 02 project partners (recipient to be verified)

**Subject:** Request for the Pared River Catchment fish checklist and supporting survey metadata

Magandang araw,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am developing Bangwit, an independent fishing-information and private catch-journal project. The app is currently a Cavite prototype; the longer-term plan is to build evidence-reviewed coverage across Luzon first, then Visayas and Mindanao, including rivers, lakes, reservoirs, brackish waters and coastal areas. National coverage is not complete, and we do not want to imply that a project summary is a current or complete species inventory.

I read the public Darwin Initiative 31-005 project reports describing the Pared River Catchment biological assessment. The Annual Report refers to Annex 9 and reports aggregate fish totals, while the public project page lists report downloads but does not list Annex 9 or Annex 12. I am writing to ask whether the following materials or summaries can be shared, and under what conditions:

1. The Annex 9 ecological assessment and its accepted fish checklist, including the names as originally recorded and the taxonomic determinations used.
2. Sampling dates, methods, effort and the waterbody, sanctuary, named-reach or station crosswalk for each fish record. Generalized locations are fine if precise sites should not be disclosed.
3. The basis for each native/invasive classification, plus voucher, specimen-photo or other verification identifiers where available.
4. A data dictionary and clarification of how the municipality-level aquatic counts relate to the report's ten native and four invasive fish totals, and to the eight native species mentioned in the tagging update.
5. Annex 12 or other authorized map products, including their CRS, whether they depict indicative study areas or legally adopted sanctuary boundaries, and any conditions on reuse or redistribution.
6. Written attribution and reuse terms for the checklist, metadata, maps and any summaries derived from them, including whether noncommercial or commercial use and translation are permitted.

We will not publish sensitive coordinates, copy maps, or add species claims to Bangwit without reviewing the source, location precision, taxonomic evidence and reuse terms. We will keep project-period observations distinct from current status and will not present sanctuary information as fishing permission unless authoritative rules and boundaries are separately verified.

If these materials are not shareable, a public report, DOI, repository record, or contact point where the results can be accessed would still be helpful. Please let me know if this request should be directed to another project partner or data custodian.

Maraming salamat,

Joseph Lopez
