# Unsent author/NFRDI clarification draft — Ibulao DNA barcode records

**Status:** Draft only. Not sent.

**To:** `tpjf@nfrdi.da.gov.ph`

**Subject:** Clarification request: Ibulao River DNA barcode accessions and sample mapping

Dear Philippine Journal of Fisheries team,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am independently developing **Bangwit**, an early-stage fishing explorer and catch journal intended to expand in phases across the Philippines, from Luzon to the Visayas and Mindanao. I am currently reviewing public research sources and building a carefully sourced research inventory. No Ibulao species or DNA-barcode rows from your article are currently published in the app.

I reviewed Tauli et al. (2022), “Fish Species Composition and Distribution from 2015 to 2019 in the Ibulao River, Ifugao, Cordillera, Philippines,” and its 2023 erratum. The erratum’s Table 2 has a `GENBANK` column heading, but the six sample rows I could extract show no accession strings. I would appreciate clarification before I decide whether any of these records can be represented in Bangwit.

Could you please advise:

1. Were GenBank accession IDs assigned or submitted for samples Ibulao 14, Ibulao 08, Ibulao 13, Ibulao 9, Ibulao 10, and Ibulao 11? If so, could you provide the accession IDs and the archive links? If not, please confirm that no accessions were deposited.
2. Is there a per-sample crosswalk identifying which sequence or tissue corresponds to each sample code, including the COI and D-loop work described in the erratum? If available for sharing, could you point me to the reference sequences/accessions, alignments, or calculation method used for the reported percent identities?
3. Are Ibulao 08 and Ibulao 13 explicitly linked to the Table 1 label `BFARCAR_Bunog02_154`, or are these separate sample records? If linked, could you share the documented mapping?
4. Are voucher/catalogue numbers, diagnostic photographs, or other records available to support the `Rhinogobius giurinus` and `Zenarchopterus philippinus` identifications? I am keeping these names held for review and will not infer specimen identity from the name-matching databases alone.
5. The article PDFs state CC BY-NC 4.0, while the erratum landing page has a general CC BY 4.0 footer. Could you clarify the applicable terms for reuse of the article’s findings and whether the underlying sequence data have separate access or reuse terms? Bangwit may eventually be publicly available and could have a revenue-generating model; I will not use noncommercial-only material in the app unless the intended use is covered by permission or a suitable license.

This is an information and rights inquiry only. I am not requesting certification, a legal determination, or that you prepare new analyses. A link to an existing data record or a brief clarification would be very helpful. If this is better directed to the authors or another NFRDI unit, please let me know the appropriate contact.

Thank you for your time.
