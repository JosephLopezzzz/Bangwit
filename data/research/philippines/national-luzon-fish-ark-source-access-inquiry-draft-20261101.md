# Draft inquiry: UPLB Fish Ark Southern Luzon freshwater guide

**Status:** Draft only; not sent. Confirm the correct recipient and add sender contact details before sending. This file intentionally contains no personal email address.

**To:** University of the Philippines Los Baños Limnological Research Station and/or Museum of Natural History

**Subject:** Inquiry about the 2011 *Freshwater Fishes of Southern Luzon* guide and Fish Ark survey records

Good day,

I am Joseph Lopez, an independent project developer working on Bangwit, a local-first fishing information and catch-journal web app. The first pilot is focused on Cavite, while the longer-term plan is to build carefully sourced fish information for the Philippines, expanding region by region across Luzon, then the Visayas and Mindanao. The project includes inland waters such as rivers, lakes, streams and reservoirs as well as coastal and marine areas. Coverage is incomplete, and I do not intend to present historical research as proof of current fish presence or as a complete official inventory.

DOST-PCAARRD's 2013 Fish Ark article describes a UPLB photoguide with 72 fish species based on a three-year survey of 28 Southern Luzon sites. I also found citations to a UPLB Limnological Research Station publication titled *Freshwater Fishes of Southern Luzon, Philippines* (2011), but references differ on the authorship and page count (91 versus 100 pages). A separate UPLB repository record lists Paolo P. Ocampo's 2011 Fish Ark terminal report and says the document is not currently available there.

Could you please help clarify or provide, if publicly shareable:

1. The correct bibliographic details, edition and relationship among the photoguide, the 2011 *Freshwater Fishes of Southern Luzon* publication and the Fish Ark terminal report.
2. A public or authorized way to consult the guide and, if available, the exact 28-site roster, field dates and methods, and site-by-taxon table.
3. Any voucher or collection accession numbers and an existing crosswalk to the survey sites and reported taxa.
4. The applicable terms for citing or reusing scientific names, locality facts, tables, photographs and descriptive text in a public, noncommercial independent project.
5. The appropriate process for requesting access if these materials are not publicly available.

I will cite the source and follow its stated conditions. I will not reproduce guide text or photographs without permission, and I will not add site-specific species claims unless the supporting locality and identification evidence can be reviewed. This is an availability and reuse inquiry only; it is not a request for paid certification, a legal boundary, or an official endorsement of the app.

Thank you for your guidance.

Sincerely,

Joseph Lopez
