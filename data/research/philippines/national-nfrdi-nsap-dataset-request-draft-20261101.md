# Draft only: request for NSAP summary data and reuse terms

**Status:** Unsent. This is a working draft, not an official request or an authorization to disclose personal or confidential data.

**To:** National Fisheries Research and Development Institute (NFRDI), through its currently listed official channel

**Subject:** Request for existing NSAP catch summaries, documentation, and reuse conditions for Bangwit research

Dear NFRDI,

My name is Joseph Lopez. I am independently developing Bangwit, a Philippines-wide fishing information project that is currently starting with a Cavite pilot. The plan is to expand region by region across Luzon, then the Visayas and Mindanao, and to cover coastal and inland waters such as bays, rivers, lakes, reservoirs, estuaries, and wetlands where suitable sources exist.

I am requesting information about existing National Stock Assessment Program (NSAP) outputs that may be shared for research and public-interest educational use. I am not asking NFRDI to conduct a new survey or create a new dataset. If available, could you provide the most recent five years of existing, non-personal, aggregate catch summaries, or advise which published reports or NSAP Interactive Atlas outputs may be used?

For any available dataset or report, it would help to receive:

1. Annual or monthly summaries by scientific taxon/name as recorded, region or fishing ground, landing site, and fishing gear, with units and the period covered.
2. The codebook or data dictionary, including the meaning of fields, gear labels, missing values, and any revisions to scientific or local names.
3. A description of the monitored sites, data collection method, site coverage by year, known gaps, and whether records refer to catch landed at a site or the location where fish were captured.
4. Any public documentation for the NSAP Interactive Atlas filters, exports, API, or available reports.
5. The conditions for attribution, redistribution, adaptation, public display in a noncommercial app, and any commercial reuse. Please identify any fields or outputs that must remain restricted.

Aggregate data are sufficient. I do not need fisher names, boat identifiers, contact details, or other personal information. If exact capture coordinates or vessel-level records are sensitive or unavailable, please omit them; a documented fishing-ground or waterbody label is sufficient for this inquiry.

Bangwit will distinguish historical landed-catch evidence from verified species occurrence, current abundance, catchability, natural diet, water safety, and current fishing rules. We will not treat landing sites as capture coordinates. Any source used in the project will be attributed, and we will follow the reuse conditions you provide. We will not publish data unless its terms allow that use.

Please let me know whether an existing report, public dashboard export, or aggregate dataset is available, the current process for requesting it, and whether additional documentation is required. No paid service, certification, or new data collection is requested; please identify any charge before any such step.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,

Joseph Lopez  
Independent developer, Bangwit
