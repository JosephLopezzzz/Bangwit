**Subject:** Inquiry about whole-country Philippine Hydrologic Dataset access and terms for Bangwit

Good day,

I am Joseph Lopez, developing Bangwit, an independent project that is building a source-linked fish information and private catch-journal web app. It began with a Cavite pilot, and the current research plan is to build regional coverage across Luzon first, then the Visayas and Mindanao. The long-term scope is the whole Philippines. The current app is an early prototype; it is not a navigation product, and its private catch journal is stored locally on a user's device.

We are looking for reliable inland-water reference data to organize source review across the country. I saw that LiPAD lists Hydrological Datasets and says that large or whole-country requests should be discussed with the Business Development Officer. Could you please advise whether Bangwit can request existing Philippine Hydrologic Dataset (PHD) products, including, where available:

- river and stream centerlines, catchment or watershed boundaries, and identifiers/names;
- available lake, reservoir, wetland, irrigation or other inland-water layers;
- coverage, data date/version, file formats, CRS/datum and metadata for each layer;
- official attribution and license/reuse terms, especially for displaying derived geometry in a public, noncommercial prototype;
- restrictions on redistribution, caching, modification or later commercial use;
- fees, eligibility requirements and the correct request process for a whole-country request; and
- required Area of Interest shapefile format, or whether a national coverage request can use an AOI supplied by your office.

I found a published reference to a broader PHD inventory of principal river basins and a separate open dataset of 128 larger catchments. I do not assume these are the same product, that PHD files are openly downloadable, or that any dataset is free to reuse. We will keep fish records and resulting public species claims tied to their own biological sources; hydrology data would be used only as spatial context.

Please let me know what project description or supporting documents you need before I submit a formal request. This is an availability and terms inquiry only; please do not begin a paid request or processing on this message.
