# Bangwit Cavite usability pilot — participant notice draft

**Status: NOT APPROVED — not for recruitment or session use.** This draft needs review by the project owner and an appropriate privacy/legal reviewer. Fill every marked item and confirm any school or institutional research-review requirements before inviting anyone.

## What the session is for

We are testing whether people can understand and use the Bangwit prototype. We are testing the interface, not the participant. The first planned round is intended for a mix of beginner and experienced fishers. The session is not a fishing-safety check, a test of fishing knowledge, or a representative survey of Cavite fishers. No participants have been recruited.

The prototype's species evidence is limited and historical. Live weather and hazard warnings are unavailable. The map does not define legal fishing boundaries, access rights, or safe conditions.

## What participation involves

You may be asked to try the first-time in-app guide and its Help/replay control, find a species and its source, explain an unavailable advisory, save a synthetic catch while offline, inspect My Species, and review a backup made from the synthetic test record. Use only the example values provided by the facilitator. Do not enter a real catch, exact fishing spot, GPS location, account credential, personal contact, or private photograph.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time without giving a reason. **Compensation:** `[project owner to specify, or state “none”]`.
