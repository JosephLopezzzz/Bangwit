# Bangwit Cavite pilot session guide

Draft facilitator material — **do not recruit or run sessions until the pilot notice and privacy/contact details are reviewed.** This guide uses synthetic data and does not request a participant's real catch, exact fishing spot, account, or GPS location.

## Session setup

- Use one fixed, recorded build and HTTPS origin for each round. Do not switch builds mid-session.
- Planned first-round audience: a mix of beginner and experienced fishers. This choice is for task planning only; no participants have been recruited.
- Use a fresh test profile with no personal journal data. Use only the app's synthetic catch, photo and note values.
- Tell the participant that Bangwit is a prototype, the Cavite species records are historical and limited, live warnings are unavailable, and the map is not a legal fishing boundary or navigation aid.
- Ask before taking notes or recording. Recording is optional and needs its own clear notice; prefer deidentified task notes without audio/video.
- Record device model, OS, browser/version, screen size, build/URL, date and whether the task was completed without help, with help, or not completed. Do not record names or precise home/fishing locations in the issue notes.

## Opening script

“Thanks for trying this Bangwit prototype. We are testing the design, not you. You can skip any task or stop at any time. Please use only the example details shown; do not enter a real catch, private fishing spot, or personal contact. The map does not confirm legal access or safe conditions, and live alerts are not enabled. Is it okay if I take brief notes about where the interface is confusing? I will not record audio or video unless we separately discuss and agree to it.”

Do not start if the participant has not received and understood the separately approved pilot notice. Do not collect a signature or consent response for the project through this draft script.

## Tasks

1. **Find a species and its source.** “Choose Cavite and find one species record. Tell me what the record does and does not prove.” Observe source-link discovery and whether the participant infers current presence, a specific bay, or permission to catch.
2. **Interpret coverage.** “What would you do if the advisory says unavailable or the last update is old?” Expected understanding: check the official source independently; missing or stale data is not an all-clear.
3. **Save a synthetic catch offline.** Use a synthetic species or unidentified value, a generated image, `50 cm`, `1000 g`, a test note, and a fake place label. Disconnect only the test device/profile, save, reopen and verify the entry and photo. Reconnect while an unsaved synthetic draft is open; verify the draft is retained and one save creates one entry.
4. **Understand My Species.** Ask the participant to distinguish identified catches from unknown catches and explain whether the collection proves the fish is present in Cavite.
5. **Back up the test journal.** Export only the synthetic test record. Ask the participant where the file would be private and how restore behaves; do not restore over a populated journal.

## Observation and thresholds

For each task, record start/end, unassisted/assisted/not completed, help given, confusion point, participant's interpretation, and a short deidentified note. Report raw counts alongside percentages. Treat any interpretation that turns unknown species evidence, unavailable warnings or map outlines into a safety/legal claim as a high-priority issue. Treat any loss of a saved synthetic catch/photo or accidental duplication as a blocker.

The plan proposes 5–8 participants and 80% unassisted completion for the core discovery, offline save and backup tasks. These are discovery targets, not a representative research sample or launch certification. The selected audience is a mix of beginner and experienced fishers; finalize task wording and complete the approved participant notice, contact, storage and review gates before recruitment. After each session, stop and fix journal loss or dangerous interpretation issues before visual polish.

## Closeout

Thank the participant, answer remaining questions, and remind them not to send real catches or locations through this research session. Store notes without names or precise spots, restrict access to the project team, and delete the temporary test profile only after confirming it contains no participant-owned journal data. Do not claim that a pilot ran until sessions and a dated deidentified report exist.
